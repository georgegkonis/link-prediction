\section{Contributions}

The main contributions of this thesis are:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item A systematic comparison of structural, semantic, and hybrid link prediction
          methods on the DSAA 2023 Wikipedia benchmark.
    \item \textbf{CascadeLP}, a confidence-based tiered inference framework that
          routes node pairs to progressively expensive models only when cheaper
          tiers are uncertain.
    \item A cold-start analysis that isolates performance on node pairs with no
          structural neighbours (zero common neighbours).
    \item An efficiency benchmark quantifying throughput and transformer call rate
          at each cascade tier.
    \item A temporal generalisation evaluation using chronological train/test splits.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Thesis Structure}

The remainder of this thesis is organised as follows.
Chapter~\ref{chap:background} reviews the relevant literature across the three
prediction paradigms.
Chapter~\ref{chap:methodology} describes the CascadeLP framework.
Chapter~\ref{chap:experiments} presents the experimental setup and results.
Chapter~\ref{chap:analysis} provides ablation studies, cold-start analysis, and
efficiency benchmarks.
Chapter~\ref{chap:conclusion} summarises the findings and outlines future directions.
